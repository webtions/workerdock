# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want WorkerDock's community to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their background or
experience. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place the project is represented.

## What we expect
- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent, and explain things kindly.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the idea, not the person.
- Stay on topic and keep it professional.
- Respect that this is a small project maintained in limited time.

## What we don't accept
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing threads, or repeatedly pushing a request after a clear "no".
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, promotion unrelated to the project, or anything illegal.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules, without
notice. To report a problem, contact the maintainer using the details in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark the message
"Conduct". Reports are handled privately.
